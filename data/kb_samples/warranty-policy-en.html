Warranty Policy

This is a summary of the Ngan Ha warranty policy effective from 1 January 2026. The Vietnamese version prevails in case of any difference.

1. Warranty periods

The warranty period starts on the invoice date for in-store purchases, or on the delivery date for online orders.

Product groupExamplesPeriod
Small home appliancesrice cookers, air fryers, blenders, microwaves12 months
Large appliancesrefrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, TVs, water purifiers24 months
Inverter compressorscompressors of inverter refrigerators and inverter air conditioners10 years
Accessoriesremote controls, detachable power cords, batteries6 months
Consumableswater filter cartridges, RO membranesnot covered

Motors of inverter washing machines are covered for 24 months like the rest of the machine; the 10-year coverage applies only to compressors.

2. Replacement within 30 days

If a manufacturing defect is confirmed within 30 days of purchase, the product is replaced with a new unit of the same model free of charge. If that model is out of stock, the customer may choose an equivalent model or a full refund.

3. What you need

  • The purchase invoice (Ngan Ha issues electronic invoices; there is no separate paper warranty card).
  • An intact, readable serial number that matches the invoice.

4. Not covered

  • Physical damage from drops or impacts, water damage, insects inside the unit.
  • Damage caused by unstable voltage, lightning or natural disasters.
  • Removed or damaged warranty seals and serial numbers.
  • Repairs by unauthorized service providers.
  • Household appliances used for commercial purposes.

5. Service options

On-site service is available for refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, water purifiers and TVs of 43 inches or larger. It is free within 20 km of the nearest Ngan Ha store; beyond that, a travel fee of 5,000 VND per extra kilometre applies. We contact you within 24 hours to schedule a visit.

Service center repairs apply to small appliances, TVs under 43 inches and accessories. You may drop the product at any Ngan Ha store.

6. Repair time

Diagnosis takes up to 3 working days. Repairs are completed within 15 working days; otherwise the product is replaced. If the same component fails for the third time during the warranty period, the product is replaced with a new one.

7. Extended warranty (Care+)

For products priced from 2,000,000 VND, Care+ 12 adds 12 months for 5% of the product price (minimum 99,000 VND) and Care+ 24 adds 24 months for 8% (minimum 169,000 VND). Care+ must be purchased within 30 days of the product purchase.

8. Help

You can check your warranty status on the Ngan Ha website or app using your invoice number or the product serial number, or ask our staff via the live chat on the website.
